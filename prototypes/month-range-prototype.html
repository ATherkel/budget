Månedsvalg: prototype

Throwaway prototype for #657. Sample data runs from jan. 2025 to okt. 2026, so November is not imported yet and a fresh visit opens on okt. 2026 (#660). Flip variants with the bar at the bottom.

A · Tidslinje: tryk og udvid

Tap a month to pick it. Tap a second month to stretch the range to it. The next tap starts over. On a desktop, shift-click also stretches.

B · Tidslinje: træk to håndtag

Drag the lower and upper handle. Tap the line to move the nearest handle there. Put both on the same month for a single month.

D · Tidslinje: tryk og udvid på én linje

The whole history fits on one line, like B. Tap a month to pick it, then tap a second month to stretch the range, like A. The next tap starts over. Or press a month, slide, and let go: where you pressed is the start and where you let go is the end. The handles can still be dragged.

C · Fra og til

Baseline: two dropdowns, no script needed. This is also what A and B fall back to without JavaScript.
