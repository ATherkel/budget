## First-release scope decision — 28 September 2026

During the ongoing #11 dashboard exploration, the maintainer explicitly approved
including **read-only budget comparison in the first usable release**. Targets
will be maintained outside the dashboard. Budget editing and forecasting remain
deferred.

This supersedes the blanket exclusion of budget targets for this bounded
capability. Other exclusions remain in effect. The prototype shows the desired
interaction; it does not settle the production budget input, effective-month and
publication/versioning contracts, or accept an earmarking/carry-forward engine.
Those must be agreed before production implementation, with financial arithmetic
owned outside presentation and the normal production TDD workflow retained.

The dashboard direction is Enkel / Avanceret, Mod budget available in Enkel,
monthly expense-budget comparison in trends, and category-filtered trends.
Diagnostics remain under Kontrol with a quiet incomplete-data note on the
everyday overview. See the continuing #11 timeline for implementation and trial
evidence.
